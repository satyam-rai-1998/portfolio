Claude Meetings brings a working draft into the meeting itself

By Satyam Rai · November 18, 2026

A meeting transcript can tell you that someone promised a rollout plan. A draft on screen lets the team correct the plan while the person who made that promise is still in the room.

What the beta can do

Anthropic announced the Claude Meetings beta on November 12, 2026, starting with Google Meet for Team and Enterprise plans. Claude appears as a participant and posts a notice when it joins. Participants can ask it to work on material during the call. Anthropic says the meeting record includes a transcript, summary and screen descriptions, and that audio is not saved. Anthropic’s Claude Meetings beta announcement.

Try the correction test

Consider a rollout discussion in which someone proposes Wednesday, then the release manager moves the date to Friday pending a supplier check. Ask the agent for a decision log with three fields: the agreed date, the owner and the open dependency. Read the draft back before the call ends. Wednesday belongs in the discussion history. Friday belongs in the plan, alongside the condition attached to it.

Where the review belongs

For a pilot, have the meeting owner approve the decision log before copying tasks into a project tracker. Count how many owners, deadlines and conditions need correction. That measures the work the agent actually saves and reveals whether it can distinguish a suggestion from a commitment.
