Case study · Invoice extraction and approval automation

How did I automate invoice data extraction with Azure AI Document Intelligence and Power Automate?

I used Azure AI Document Intelligence to extract structured billing data from incoming invoice documents. Power Automate carries the extracted values into the invoice processing and approval workflow.

What happens after extraction?

Extraction and approval solve different problems. The extraction step turns a document into values a flow can use. The approval step applies conditional logic to decide where the record goes next. My work on the wider automation suite included Dataverse data modeling, cloud flows and Microsoft Graph integrations.

What did the project measure?

The workflow processed 500+ invoices per week. The project reported 95% extraction accuracy. Weekly volume describes throughput. The evaluation sample size, scoring method and manual-review criteria are undocumented in the available project record, so the accuracy figure cannot establish how many invoices passed without review.
